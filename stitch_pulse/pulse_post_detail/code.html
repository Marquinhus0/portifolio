Are we losing the tactile grain in computational art?

Why the return to monochromatic physical print holds lessons for digital canvas tools. The smooth vectors of modern raster engines eliminate the friction that historically disciplined our aesthetic choices. When every gradient is frictionless, design loses its resistance.

In offset lithography, ink density fights the tooth of cotton substrate. That material dispute defines the threshold where an artist terminates refinement. By eliminating structural threshold points in generative UI algorithms, we surrender texture in favor of anodyne procedural perfection.
